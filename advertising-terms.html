These terms describe the standard scope of our one-time Meta advertising packages. The written order confirmation sets the agreed creative deliverables, fee, ad budget, campaign duration and delivery timeline. If the two differ, we clarify them before work begins.

Service & budgets7-day managementApprovalsRetargetingPayments & refunds

01 — Scope of service

Each one-time package includes the creative production or review stated on its detail page, standard Meta campaign setup, basic targeting, copy and CTA. A client may choose to launch one ad or, where suitable approved creatives are available, two ads within the agreed campaign setup. Two ads do not mean two separate campaigns. Boost Duo (Rs. 7,000) uses two existing client-approved ad creatives without new production. Creative Duo (Rs. 10,000) includes one newly designed branded static ad creative; a second ad using one eligible existing client-supplied creative is optional within the stated agency fee, and does not include producing another new creative. The client pays the Meta advertising spend separately regardless of whether one or two ads run. Monthly ongoing management is a separate service.

02 — Client media budget

All listed prices are agency service fees in Sri Lankan rupees and exclude the Meta advertising budget. The client pays Meta media spend separately, normally through the client's own ad account. The approved total Meta spend, campaign start and end dates, chosen number of ads and launch scope must be confirmed before launch. Platform taxes or additional charges, where applicable, are separate.

03 — First seven days of management

We provide basic checks and limited optimization during the first seven days after launch. After that, we do not routinely monitor, optimize or report on the campaign under the one-time service, even if the scheduled advertising period continues.

04 — Running beyond seven days

The client may choose a longer campaign period before launch. Ads can continue automatically to their approved end date using the approved budget. We do not schedule indefinite campaigns without an agreed limit. The client retains the right to pause or stop their own advertising.

05 — Creative deliverables

Existing creatives must be supplied or authorized by the client and pass a suitability check. Standard new videos are around 60 seconds, with one approved concept and one minor consolidated revision per new creative. On-location filming, additional languages, complex motion work and major reshoots require separate pricing.

06 — Production timeline

Project timing is confirmed after payment, complete assets and required access are received. Client delays in providing details or approving work may change the delivery or launch date. Campaign dates are reconfirmed if necessary.

07 — Reviews and approvals

Clients must confirm names, contact details, offers, prices, factual claims and creatives before launch. We correct our own material production or setup errors without treating them as paid change requests; a new client-directed brief or post-approval change may require a new quote.

08 — Meta access and policy compliance

Clients keep ownership and administrative control of their business pages, ad accounts, payment methods and data. We work with approved role-based permissions. All campaigns are subject to Meta review, eligibility and platform policies; rejected or restricted ads may require changes or cannot run.

09 — Advanced audience setup

Packages priced at Rs. 20,000 and above include a custom-audience/retargeting setup only where there is lawful, eligible source data, sufficient audience availability, appropriate permissions and a feasible campaign objective. We cannot promise audience size, delivery or improved results. If not feasible, we explain this before launch.

10 — Reporting and results

Boost Duo does not include a performance summary. Detailed reports are not standard deliverables for the other one-time packages unless separately agreed. We provide a management handover confirmation. No specific reach, lead volume, sales outcome or return on ad spend is guaranteed.

11 — Repeat campaigns and further work

Reusing two suitable existing creatives can be purchased as a new Boost Duo order. Additional newly designed ad creatives, new videos, new objectives, relaunches and major changes require a new order or separate written quotation. Choosing an optional second ad from an already-approved existing creative under Creative Duo does not create a second new-design deliverable. No lifetime management is included.

12 — Payments, cancellation and refunds

The agency fee, payment schedule and any applicable cancellation/refund arrangement must be stated in the individual order confirmation before payment. Undelivered or substantially defective work will be reviewed fairly under the agreed terms and applicable law. Meta media spend already consumed may not be recoverable.

13 — Intellectual property and AI content

Clients should have rights to assets supplied to us. Client usage rights for final approved creatives will be confirmed in the order; third-party music, stock or AI-generated assets may have licensing restrictions. Synthetic material must not misleadingly depict a client's real location, results or testimonials.

14 — Support and privacy

Use the verified Otter & Co. contact channel for requests. We use client details only to manage enquiries and service delivery, with appropriate access protections. These terms may be revised for future orders; the written terms agreed for an existing order remain the starting point for that order.
