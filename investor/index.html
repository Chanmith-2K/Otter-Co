Kawshalya — international sales lead

Handle English enquiries, lead qualification, discovery calls and proposal discussion. Track booked calls, close rate, and signed monthly retainers.

03 / Deliver

Production from Sri Lanka

Founders scope remote ad management and creative output; agree client-supplied footage or separately charged on-location production. Sell in client-appropriate terms and currency.

International sales economics: Before spending, validate potential customer-acquisition cost, payment collection, time-zone coverage, platform access, contracts, invoicing, applicable tax treatment and any necessary foreign-market advertising compliance. Kawshalya's commission, if agreed, should apply to collected agency service fees rather than client media budgets.

06 / Investment choices

One investment.
Two possible paths.

Present both options transparently to Kawshalya. These are negotiation illustrations, not legally binding terms, approved valuations, or guaranteed returns.

OPTION A

Fixed-return financing

Investor receives a contractually agreed repayment. No permanent company shares.

The total extra LKR 75K is 18.75% of principal over the full arrangement, not an annual interest rate. Dates, grace period, default terms and credit risks must be negotiated. Fixed repayments can strain cash flow.

OPTION B

Long-term equity partner

Investor assumes equity risk, with the potential for dividends and value growth instead of fixed loan repayment.

Dividends are never assured and depend on legally permitted distributions. Capital could be partly or fully lost. The valuation is a proposed negotiating figure, not an independent appraisal.
