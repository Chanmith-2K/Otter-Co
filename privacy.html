WEBSITE NOTICE

Privacy &
your information.

This notice describes the current public website's enquiry preparation and self-assessment tools. It is not a promise that future features will use exactly the same data practices.

Website enquiry form

The current enquiry form prepares text in your browser for you to copy and send yourself. Completing or pressing the form's button does not automatically deliver your details to Otter & Co. The website does not presently store these enquiry submissions in an agency database.

Free page self-assessment

Your answers and score are calculated in your browser. You can copy your summary. The self-assessment is not connected to Facebook or Instagram accounts and does not automatically send your answers to the agency.

Website hosting and external services

The website is hosted on Vercel and loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Hosting providers and external resources may process technical request information, including IP addresses, for delivery, security and operations according to their own policies. We do not claim that simply visiting the website leaves no technical records.

Information you choose to share

When you manually send an enquiry using a separate messaging or email service, that service handles the message under its own privacy terms. Only share information needed for the business enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, bank card details or account recovery codes.

Future updates

If we introduce online submissions, CRM integrations, analytics cookies or additional tracking, this notice should be updated before those features go live, and any applicable consent or regulatory requirements should be reviewed.

Questions or corrections

Contact Otter & Co. via its verified business communication channel to ask about submitted information or this notice. The agency should publish its verified contact details before the website is advertised publicly.
