05 / Common questions

Good to know.

Will my ads stop after day seven?

Not necessarily. They can run to the pre-approved end date using the approved total media budget. Otter & Co.'s included management ends after day seven.

Is Meta advertising spend included?

No. The one-time fee pays for agency services. Meta ad spend is separate and should remain under your business's control.

Can I use this package again?

Yes. If you already own two appropriate creatives, Boost Duo can be used for a repeat campaign. New production is covered by the relevant package.

Can I request changes after launch?

Client-requested new strategies, creatives or campaign changes after approval are separately quoted. Necessary corrections for agency errors are handled fairly.
