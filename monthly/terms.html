Monthly service
conditions.

These terms clarify our published Monthly Marketing service outline. The accepted written quotation and order confirmation should define final scope and commercial conditions.

1. Monthly service scope

GROW and SCALE are the currently offered monthly plans. Each includes its stated monthly creative quantities, Meta campaign management, publishing and reporting. GROW (Rs. 35,000/month) includes two reels (one presenter and one AI-assisted), four posts, two stories and up to two managed Meta campaigns. SCALE (Rs. 50,000/month) includes four reels, six posts, four stories and up to three managed Meta campaigns. Monitoring and optimisation are provided throughout the agreed monthly service period, according to each selected plan.

2. Agency fees and advertising spend

The published monthly package fee is separate from Meta media spend, taxes and any separately quoted services. Advertising spend is paid through the customer's approved ad account and payment method.

3. Delivery and approvals

Clients supply accurate offers, creative assets, account permissions and timely approvals. The production calendar, campaign objectives, reporting periods and any special needs should be confirmed in writing.

4. Creative scope and additional work

Current standard monthly reels are up to 45 seconds with two minor revisions. Extra content, complex production, travel, on-location shoots, inbox management and advanced technical tracking are subject to separate written pricing.

5. Reporting and performance

Reports and review sessions follow the package deliverables. Campaign outcomes are not guaranteed. Advertising results are affected by budget, the offer, competition, audience, lead response and platform rules.

6. Cancellation, billing and renewal

Payment timing, renewal arrangements, cancellation notice, refunds and work already completed must be stated in the signed quotation or order confirmation before the service begins. No universal cancellation period is represented here.

7. Client accounts and data

Clients keep ownership of their social and ad accounts, payment methods and their own business data. Otter & Co. uses approved access permissions and follows the applicable platform policies.
