06 / Good to know

Questions?
Answered.

A few things worth knowing before we work together.

Is the advertising budget included?

No. Our package price is the Otter & Co. service fee. Your advertising budget is paid separately to Meta through your business's ad account.

Do you offer content-only packages?

Our monthly plans are designed for businesses that want paid advertising alongside creative production. Standalone creative work may be quoted for suitable projects on request.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No ethical advertising agency can guarantee an exact result. We plan, test, monitor and report on campaign performance, but results depend on your offer, competition, budget and customer experience.

Do you manage TikTok campaigns too?

TikTok services are not included in our monthly packages. They can be assessed on request, subject to ad-account eligibility and a separate quote.

Who owns my ad account and data?

You do. Your Facebook Page, Instagram account, Meta ad account and business data remain yours. We work with appropriate access permissions.

What is included in a standard reel?

One approved concept, basic script, up to 45 seconds of standard editing and subtitles. Presenter filming is subject to an agreed setup and schedule. Travel, major reshoots and complex production are quoted separately.
