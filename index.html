What every one-time package includes

Two Meta ads: Two creatives/variations, usually within one campaign, with basic targeting, setup, copy and CTA.

First seven days: Basic campaign monitoring and agreed adjustments only; no ongoing monthly management.

Flexible run duration: Campaign can continue after day seven to the pre-approved end date and total Meta budget, without continued agency monitoring.

Advanced audience, where eligible: Packages Rs. 20,000 and above include one custom audience/retargeting setup if suitable data, permissions and delivery conditions are available.

Meta media spend: Paid separately by the client. Agency fees do not include ad spend or promise specific sales or lead counts.

Repeat campaigns: Already-owned eligible creatives can be reused under Boost Duo. New creative production and later changes require a new paid order.

Video scope: Around one minute for new videos, one approved concept and one minor creative revision; studio presenter only. Extra footage, travel and reshoots quoted separately.

Reporting: Boost Duo does not include a performance summary. We confirm the seven-day management handover; detailed reporting is not included by default.

06 / Good to know

Questions?
Answered.

A few things worth knowing before we work together.

Is the advertising budget included?

No. Our package price is the Otter & Co. service fee. Your advertising budget is paid separately to Meta through your business's ad account.

Where can I read the one-time advertising terms?

Each package has a detailed page, and you can read our One-Time Advertising Terms & Conditions before ordering.

How do one-time advertising packages work?

One-time packages include two Meta ad creatives, campaign setup and basic management for the first seven days. Your campaign may keep running to your pre-approved end date and budget, without ongoing agency monitoring. Meta ad spend is separate. New edits, relaunches and longer management are charged separately.

Do you offer content-only packages?

Our monthly plans are designed for businesses that want paid advertising alongside creative production. Standalone creative work may be quoted for suitable projects on request.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No ethical advertising agency can guarantee an exact result. We plan, test, monitor and report on campaign performance, but results depend on your offer, competition, budget and customer experience.

Do you manage TikTok campaigns too?

TikTok services are not included in our monthly packages. They can be assessed on request, subject to ad-account eligibility and a separate quote.

Who owns my ad account and data?

You do. Your Facebook Page, Instagram account, Meta ad account and business data remain yours. We work with appropriate access permissions.

What is included in a standard reel?

One approved concept, basic script, up to 45 seconds of standard editing and subtitles. Presenter filming is subject to an agreed setup and schedule. Travel, major reshoots and complex production are quoted separately.
