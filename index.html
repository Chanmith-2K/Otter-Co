FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is Meta ad spend included in the price?

No. Every listed price is an agency service fee. You pay Meta for your advertising spend separately.

What is the difference between One-Time and Monthly?

One-Time packages cover a defined campaign setup and creative combination. Monthly plans are ongoing marketing services, including the agreed content and campaign-management scope.

Can I use content I already have?

Yes. Boost Duo uses two approved existing ad creatives. Creative Duo includes one newly designed branded ad creative, with an optional second ad using a suitable existing creative. Other packages combine new and existing content.

Do you guarantee sales or leads?

No. Results depend on your offer, budget, business response, audience and competition. We do not promise a fixed return.

Where can I read the full conditions?

See our separate One-Time Terms and Monthly Terms.
